SOURCES AND METHODOLOGY

Evidence before certainty.

FireTrace brings together observations on real terrain. It is a prototype without operational validation: it does not recommend evacuation routes or predict spread.

Terrain
Terrarium elevation tiles from AWS Open Data. Initial vertical scale 1×.
Base map
Esri World Imagery and OpenStreetMap. This is not live imagery.
Thermal hotspots
NASA FIRMS · VIIRS S-NPP, last 7 days. The detection is shown, not the burned area. Nominal resolution does not equate to geographic accuracy.
Perimeters
WFIGS Interagency: original geometry and publication date. They are hidden if they are later than the time cursor.
Cameras
ALERTCalifornia: position, orientation and a link to its viewer. We do not download or ingest its video into VAST.
NVIDIA vision
Cosmos analyses only the image the operator selects. The text is not segmentation or geolocation. YOLO11 COCO is not used as a fire detector.
Visual references
FireTrace by Dilum Sanjaya; World Wildfire Locator by nagix; fire-progression-NRTDV by J-mazz. Original implementation.

Import: FeatureCollection with Point, LineString, Polygon or MultiPolygon. Each feature requires properties.observedAt (ISO 8601 with time zone) and may include properties.source. Imports are kept for the session only.
