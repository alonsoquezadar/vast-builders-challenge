How it is calculated

Minimum arrival time between 50 m cells (8 neighbours). Rate = base × fuel × W × S, with W = exp(0.06·v·cos Δ) according to the bearing relative to the direction the wind blows towards and S = exp(3·slope) limited to 0.25–4. The coefficients are heuristic and unvalidated. No moisture, spotting or suppression. The shape is biased by the grid.
